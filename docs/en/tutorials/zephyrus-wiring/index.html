Zephyrus Gyro Wiring

Wire an MPU6050 to the I2C bus, resolve pin conflicts and verify the AHRS with the serial log.

I2C pin selection

The default Zephyrus I2C pins are <strong>GPIO4 (SDA)</strong> and <strong>GPIO5 (SCL)</strong>. On the PWMP7 v1.0 those are fine; on <strong>v1.1</strong> GPIO2/GPIO5 are not broken out, so Zephyrus uses <strong>GPIO1 (SDA, CH2)</strong> and <strong>GPIO3 (SCL, CH3)</strong>. Always check the silkscreen against the pin map — GPIO4 is hard-reserved for the SX1280 radio interrupt and must never be repurposed.

Wiring the GY-521

GY-521 Receiver
VCC 5V rail (onboard regulator makes 3.3V)
GND GND
SDA GPIO1 (CH2) — or GPIO5 on v1.0
SCL GPIO3 (CH3) — or GPIO2 on v1.0
AD0 / INT / XDA / XCL leave open

The GY-521 carries its own 4.7&nbsp;kΩ pull-ups and an AMS1117-3.3V regulator: feed it 5V, it makes its own 3.3V and the logic levels auto-match. <strong>Four wires total</strong>, no trace cutting, no extra regulator.

Calibration & verification

The Mahony AHRS self-calibrates the gyro bias at boot — keep the airframe <em>still</em> for the first second after power-up. Verify the sensor is alive via the serial log: pitch/roll should track the airframe when you tilt it, and the crest rudder should oppose a manual roll (right roll → rudder corrects left). If the bus hangs at boot, you've landed on a reserved pin — re-check the map and move to the correct CH2/CH3 pair.
