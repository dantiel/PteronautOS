Tutorial 08

WiFi Flashing with the Pteronaut-Egg

Flash and update entirely over WiFi — no cable, no FTDI, no UART, ever.

The Pteronaut-Egg is a tiny WiFi bootstrap (~294 KB) that lives on the receiver. Its only job is to accept the full PteronautOS firmware over WiFi and stage it — so you never need a cable at all.

The ESP8285 has a single ~1 MB flash region. A full image (~636 KB) cannot fit its own free staging space, so it cannot update itself over WiFi. The egg is small enough to fit, and the full image is small enough to flash the egg back — closing an endless egg ↔ full cycle.

01 — Get the egg

Download the prebuilt Pteronaut-Egg image. It is a fixed, config-independent bootstrap — no settings, no build required.

Download pteronaut-egg.bin.gz

02 — Get the full firmware

The full firmware is always built fresh in the Fossil Etcher — pick your receiver, mixer and config, then download the newest image straight from the browser. No PlatformIO.

Open the Fossil Etcher

The Etcher builds current PteronautOS from source every time, so you always get the latest firmware with best-practice defaults.

03 — No flashing needed

Your receiver ships with the Pteronaut-Egg already on it, so there is nothing to flash and no cable is ever needed. If the egg is ever wiped, you can restore it over WiFi from the full firmware’s WebUI.

04 — Connect to the egg

Power the receiver. The egg opens a WiFi access point; join it, then open the egg’s upload page in a browser.

Field Value
WiFi network (AP) PTERONAUT-EGG
WiFi password hatchnatural
Upload page (mDNS, with 192.168.4.1 fallback) pteronaut-egg.local

05 — Upload the full firmware

On the egg’s upload page, drag the full firmware you downloaded from the Fossil Etcher onto the ASCII egg. The egg stages it and reboots into full PteronautOS.

06 — Every future update

From now on, updating is two WiFi uploads and zero cables. Full PteronautOS runs the standard ELRS WebUI — open its Firmware Update page, upload the egg back, then upload the new full firmware. The cycle repeats indefinitely.

Brick risk (honest)

Like any over-the-air flash: only a broken full image whose WiFi never starts would force a wired recovery. In normal use the egg removes the cable entirely — no cable, ever.

The Fossil Etcher is the shared flashing tool for both WiFi and UART workflows — download the egg, or build the full firmware directly, all from the browser.
