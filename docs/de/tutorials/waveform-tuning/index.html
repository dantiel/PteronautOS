Wellenform-Tuning

Forme den Flügelschlag mit Heftigkeit, Amplitude, Frequenz und Gleitwinkel passend zu Flügel und Zelle.

Explore the parameters live with the ferocity waveform explorer before touching the firmware — the same model drives the mixer.

Heftigkeit (Schlag-Asymmetrie)

Ferocity is the stroke/return asymmetry. At 0 the wave is a pure sine — equal time up and down. Raising down-ferocity makes the downstroke faster and the upstroke dwell longer (and vice-versa for up-ferocity). This shifts where thrust is produced: a fast downstroke + slow recovery mimics a real bird's power stroke and is the single most effective feel change.

Start at down 8, up 0 for a powerful downstroke, then blend up-ferocity in small steps. Too much asymmetry makes the wings snap at reversal — audible as a click and visible as servo heat.

Amplitude und Frequenz

Amplitude is not set directly; it emerges from frequency and stroke speed. Raising the flap frequency shortens the stroke for a given speed, lowering it lengthens the stroke. The kernel caps amplitude so the servo never leaves the 988–2012 µs envelope. Tune frequency for the airframe's natural flap resonance — a too-high frequency wastes energy, a too-low one drops below effective lift.

The shape mix slider blends the pure sine toward a trapezoid (more dwell at the reversal points). Higher mix = crisper reversals and more thrust at the extremes; lower mix = smoother, more efficient cruise.

Gleit- und Schlagwinkel

Glide angle and flapping angle are both centre offsets (int8, ±15°), not amplitude. Glide sets the common wing angle when the throttle commands glide; flapping shifts the flap stroke's centre. They let you bias the wing for slow flight vs. fast cruise without touching the mechanical linkage.

Start-Presets

Preset down / up mix Character
Extreme 8 / 0 100% Hard power stroke, aggressive
Near-sine 7 / 1 100% Balanced, most natural
High dwell 8 / 6 100% Long reversal dwell, floaty
Mixed 8 / 6 50% Soft blend, cruise efficiency

Begin with Near-sine, confirm stable flight, then move toward Extreme only if you want punch. Every airframe has a different sweet spot — change one parameter at a time.
